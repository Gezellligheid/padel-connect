-- Needed features

beforehand, people will need to create or login into an account, this will be done via a communicating-ish way. Think of it as an onboarding of a wel known dating app with questions like:  
"Hi, how should people call you" or "tell us your age", and so on.

The login page is directly the pincode of the user. A new user can be made on /register

The whole app is like a  tinder for Padel. Whenever a user creates an account, they will be asked at a certain point to enter their free time that they (want to) spend a week on padel. With this data (the available padeltime) they will be matched in sets of 4 poeple that have similar play preferences.  
This matching starts whenever the management team of the event starts a round on a private page let us say.

When starting a match, the users will see a screen who (only name and profile pic) and on whitch field they are playing. The field numbers are setable on the management page. The duo teams are chosen randomly. 

Whenever a certain won matches have arrived, the group can click the "finish match" button, when all of them are clicked. their match will be stopped. The number of amount matches to play has to be set in management. When the match is over. you can click a connect button next to the people you want to connect with. this will add the person to your friends list, that you can check via a menu. There their number and playtimes etc can be found.

Again then, a new round can be started by the management. This will create new groups of 4 and the cycle restarts.

name, a pincode (4 digits) for simple authentication, phone number, hobbys (think of pills that can be selected, and ofcourse the playtime preferences.
